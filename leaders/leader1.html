BIC Hackathon

BIC Hackathon is a time bounded event organized by Boston Center for IT in collaboration with Boston International College. In this event, teams collaborate to develop & build a prototype, and pitch ideas. The best three teams will be rewarded.

IT Club IT Bahas

Digital Bahas by Boston Center for IT is an interactive event focused on tech discussions, digital trends, and IT insights. It brings together professionals and learners to share knowledge and explore new ideas in the digital world.

FreeCodeCamp

FreeCodeCamp, organized by Boston Center for IT, provides collaborative support and opportunities to learn coding. It offers free workshops and projects where you can work with others, build skills, and grow your tech network.
